About this work

Pair of Japanese landscape woodblock prints featuring Utagawa Hiroshige’s Fujieda and Kanaya designs from the Kyōka edition of The Fifty-three Stations of the Tōkaidō.

These detailed scenes depict travelers crossing rivers, porters carrying passengers and goods, and wooded Japanese landscapes. Soft blue, green, and earth tones are complemented by Japanese inscriptions and red title cartouches.

Includes both unframed prints pictured. The prints appear consistent with woodblock printing, with paper texture and visible color showing through the reverse.

Condition: Visible creasing, wrinkles, paper toning, and edge and corner wear. Please review the original front and reverse photographs closely for the condition of each sheet.

Sold together as a lot of two. Will be carefully packed flat with rigid protection for shipping.
