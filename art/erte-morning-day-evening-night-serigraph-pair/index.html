Up for acquisition is the quintessential two-piece suite from the "Father of Art Deco," Romain de Tirtoff (Erté), usually retailing at $20,000 for the set. This set features his world-renowned masterpieces "Morning, Day" and "Evening, Night," produced in 1982-1985.

Both pieces are hand-signed by the artist and share the matching limited edition number 182/300. This primary edition is highly coveted for its vibrant colors, intricate embossing, and luxurious foil stamping on heavy black wove paper.
